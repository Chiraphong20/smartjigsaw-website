######## A) ภาพเดสก์ท็อป — สัดส่วนตอน Gen 21:9 แล้วครอปบน-ล่างเป็น 2400×840 (ให้จุดเด่นอยู่กึ่งกลางแนวตั้ง) ########
Bright, friendly product-marketing banner illustration in a polished 3D render style (soft glossy clay-like materials, soft studio lighting, subtle gradient background, small decorative dotted patterns and floating soft shapes).
Layout (very important): ultra-wide banner, aspect ratio 21:9. The LEFT 48% of the frame is a calm, empty, light gradient area reserved for a big headline, subtitle and bullet text that will be added later in a design tool.
The product scene sits on the RIGHT 52%, vertically centered, with some elements overlapping toward the middle.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no readable UI text (use abstract blocks and icons on screens).
Background tone: warm yellow-to-orange gradient (#FFE08A to #FFB347) with blue (#2DA6DD) and navy (#131C45) accents
Scene: a modern POS touchscreen monitor with a receipt printer and a handheld barcode scanner, a smartphone showing an abstract sales dashboard (bar chart icons, no readable text), small floating badges (QR code, card, stock box), and the cute 3D brown owl mascot as a cashier giving a thumbs-up.
